Merhaba! I'm a Ph.D. student at the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu), working with [Dr. Katie Skinner](https://robotics.umich.edu/profile/katherine-skinner/) at the [Field Robotics Group](https://fieldrobotics.engin.umich.edu/). I'm interested in applying probabilistic learning methods for computer vision and robotics to tasks such as state estimation, mapping, and decision-making in challenging environments.

I previously worked at [iRobot](https://www.irobot.com/), and have interned at the [University of Washington Applied Physics Lab](https://www.apl.uw.edu/) as a visiting researcher, advised by [Dr. Aaron Marburg](https://www.apl.washington.edu/people/profile.php?last_name=Marburg&first_name=Aaron).

## Research Interests

- Probabilistic learning for robot perception
- Underwater robot state estimation and mapping
- Sensor fusion (sonar, vision, acoustic)
- Novel view synthesis and implicit neural representations
- Decision-making under uncertainty
