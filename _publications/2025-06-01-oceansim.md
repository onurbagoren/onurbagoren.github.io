**Authors:** Jingyu Song, Haoyu Ma, Onur Bagoren, Advaith Sethuraman, Yiting Zhang, Katherine A. Skinner

**Venue:** IROS 2025

Developing and testing underwater perception algorithms requires extensive real-world data collection, which is expensive and difficult. OceanSim provides a GPU-accelerated simulation framework with realistic underwater rendering for sonar, cameras, and other sensors.
